Harsh works mainly in Python. For agents he uses LangGraph, LangChain and MCP with NeMo Guardrails; for retrieval Qdrant, FAISS, ChromaDB and HNSW indexes with cross-encoder reranking and RAGAS evaluation; for models Groq and Google Gemini; for serving FastAPI; and PyTorch, TensorFlow and scikit-learn for modelling. He deploys with Docker, Google Cloud Run, Render, Hugging Face Spaces and Vercel, with GitHub Actions CI and Redis caching.
