Warrant is an AI customer-support agent that must justify every action with evidence before it acts. Harsh engineered it with a hand-rolled state machine, confidence thresholds, policy-based approval tokens, and a reconciler that detects forged tokens. It was built at the Swytchcode x Knotic hackathon at the Paytm office.
